## Command index

Setup:

- `python tools/start.py --smoke --tag k10d`
- `python -m pytest tests/test_k10d_served.py -q -p no:cacheprovider`

Demo:

- `python tools/start.py --smoke --tag k10d-demo --data-dir DATA`
- `python tools/start.py --serve --tag k10d-demo --data-dir DATA`

Fixture:

- `python -m pytest tests/test_k10d_served.py -q -p no:cacheprovider -k fixture`
- `python -m pytest tests/test_k10d_served.py -q -p no:cacheprovider`

The `tools/start.py --smoke` setup entry runs the checkout, configure,
lockfiles and launch steps on a fresh profile and records sha256 hashes
plus the smoke result.

## Analytics evidence

Totals come only from the authoritative store file
(`telemetry_store.json`): `cloud_calls` plus `tokens_total` summed by
metric name, deterministically ordered, identical across repeated reads.
Per-model and per-provider views carry calls plus tokens plus cost, with
`UNAVAILABLE` wherever no MEASURED numeric row exists (never zero).
Fabricated totals that are not in the store are rejected by
`verify_totals_against_store`.

## Fixture evidence

`tests/fixtures/k10d_fixtures.json` rows are labelled `TEST FIXTURES`
with provenance stating they are never measured evidence; their ids never
appear among MEASURED store rows. Unlabelled rows fail
`validate_fixture_rows`.
